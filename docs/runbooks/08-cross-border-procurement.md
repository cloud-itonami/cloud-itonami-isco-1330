# Cross-border procurement and shipment

1. Identify exact SKU/components, origin, destination, consignee, end-user/use and all intermediaries.
2. Obtain origin export classification/licence decision and destination import classification/licence decision from accountable reviewers.
3. Screen every party and beneficial owner against applicable restrictions immediately before commitment and shipment; record list/version/time.
4. Name exporter/importer of record, customs broker, forwarder, Incoterms rule/place/version, title/risk point and cargo insurer.
5. Freeze serials, packing, tamper evidence, route, handoffs and exception contacts. A route or consignee change stops shipment pending renewed review.
6. Preserve commercial invoice, packing list, origin, licence, customs, insurance and custody records.
7. At receipt, reconcile seal/serial/condition before acceptance. Quarantine discrepancies and notify insurer/vendor within policy deadlines.
8. Remote support or telemetry crossing borders follows the separately approved data-transfer pack; hardware clearance does not approve data transfer.
