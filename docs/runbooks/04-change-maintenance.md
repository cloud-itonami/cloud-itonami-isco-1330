# Change and maintenance

Create a change record containing purpose, assets/tenants, risk, test evidence,
implementation steps, maintenance window, communications, executor, approver,
rollback trigger and rollback steps. Resolve conflicts and verify backups.

At execution, confirm the approved version, announce start, capture before-state,
perform one controlled step at a time and record evidence. Stop at an unexpected
result. Validate health and tenant service, announce completion and close only
after monitoring. Emergency changes receive retrospective review.
