# Decommission and disposal

1. Confirm signed termination/retirement approval, asset ownership, tenant migration and retention obligations.
2. Stop scheduling, notify tenants, reconcile final usage and verify approved exports/backups.
3. Remove service credentials and keys; approved shutdown and de-energisation only.
4. Sanitise each medium using the contractually selected standard and record serial, method, tool result, witness and exception. Destroy failed media through an approved provider.
5. Remove, pack and transfer assets with chain of custody. Update inventory, warranty, insurance and financial records.
6. Obtain destruction/resale/recycling certificates and close only after all data, access, billing and open incidents are reconciled.
