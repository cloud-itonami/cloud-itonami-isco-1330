# Procure to rack

1. Freeze requirements: workload, GPU count/memory, network, storage, power, cooling, delivery and security.
2. Issue approved RFQ to at least the required number of qualified suppliers; retain exactly what was sent.
3. Validate vendor identity, authority, sanctions/export restrictions, warranty channel and quote validity.
4. Normalise currency, tax, freight, installation, support, warranty, lead time and power; record exceptions rather than silently scoring them.
5. Obtain budget, technical, security and legal approvals; then issue PO. Never accept substitutions without repeat review.
6. Before delivery, verify rack units, floor load, A/B feeds, breaker capacity, connector, cooling envelope, network ports, loading route and insurance.
7. On receipt, photograph seals, reconcile serials, quarantine anomalies and preserve chain of custody.
8. Rack and cable to `[SITE-SPECIFIC METHOD]`; peer-check labels, grounding, A/B feeds and management isolation.
9. Approved energisation only. Apply firmware baseline, rotate credentials, run diagnostics/burn-in and collect evidence.
10. Execute acceptance certificate; unresolved failures remain conditionally accepted or rejected. Register asset, warranty and maintenance dates.
