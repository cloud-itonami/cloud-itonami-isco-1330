# Incident response

1. Detect and assign severity; start an immutable timeline and incident commander.
2. Protect people first and follow `[SITE EMERGENCY PROCEDURE]` for fire, electrical, coolant or physical hazards.
3. Identify affected tenants/assets and preserve logs. Do not speculate in customer communications.
4. Containment that powers down racks, changes cooling, isolates tenant networks or rotates credentials requires named approval unless the site emergency rule explicitly authorises immediate action.
5. Notify stakeholders using contractual timelines; separate confirmed facts, impact, action and next update.
6. Recover from known-good configuration, validate service and monitor recurrence.
7. Within `[N]` business days, issue a blameless review with cause, contributing controls, evidence, corrective owner and due date.
