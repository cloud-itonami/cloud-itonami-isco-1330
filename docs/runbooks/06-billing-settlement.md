# Metering, billing and settlement

1. Freeze the period and ingest signed/source-identified compute and energy meter records.
2. Reconcile scheduler allocation, downtime exclusions, contract rate/version and credits.
3. Produce an exception report for gaps, duplicates, clock drift and disputed jobs.
4. Calculate realised revenue and itemised operating costs; keep forecasts separate.
5. A second reviewer approves invoice and any owner revenue distribution before external issue/payment.
6. Record invoice ID, tax treatment supplied by the accountable adviser, payment state and ledger references. Never manufacture tax eligibility or guaranteed yield.
