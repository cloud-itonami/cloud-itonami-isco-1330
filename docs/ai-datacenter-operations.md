# AI Data Center Operations Manager (ISCO-08 1330)

This occupation actor operates the physical and logical service behind the
ISIC 6310 AI data-center business lane.

It observes rack inventory, GPU health, job capacity, energy, cooling,
incidents and maintenance. It may automatically record observations and
recommend actions. Energising or de-energising a rack, dispatching hardware,
changing cooling setpoints, rotating tenant credentials, data migration and
data-center power cycling always interrupt for named human approval.

Hard holds remain unchanged: an unregistered client or an adviser proposal
that attempts direct actuation cannot be approved. Every committed operation
is written to the existing append-only ledger.

## Responsibility split

| ISIC 6310 business actor | ISCO 1330 occupation actor |
|---|---|
| customer, contract, asset and settlement lifecycle | facility and compute operations |
| records external tax/compliance evidence | records telemetry, incidents and work |
| approves capital and commercial commitments | approves safety/security-sensitive actions |
| calculates realised period economics | supplies measured usage and operating-cost facts |
